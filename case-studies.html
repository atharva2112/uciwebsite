Rail & Transportation

Welded Cabins and Bogie Frames to EN 15085-2 CL1

What we supply

Fabricated and machined structures for global transportation companies, national railway and metro projects, and locomotive manufacturers.

  • Main and auxiliary cabins
  • Front and rear end upper frames
  • Headstocks
  • Bogie frames
  • Consoles
  • Antenna brackets

How we make it

Welded to EN 15085-2 CL1 and DIN EN ISO 3834-2 by welders qualified to EN standards, then machined, finished and inspected. Parts weigh from 0.5 kg to 4,500 kg (about 1.1 lb to 9,900 lb).
