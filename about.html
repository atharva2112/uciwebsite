About us

Who We Are

Ultra Corpotech Inc., in Conroe, Texas, is the American subsidiary of Ultra Corpotech Pvt Ltd, an engineering and contract manufacturing group founded in Pune, India, in 1991. We give customers in the United States direct access to the group’s one-stop manufacturing, with a partner in their own market.

Built on a Vision for Great Things

The group began in Pune in 1991 as Ultra Engineers, founded by Ashok N. Bhosle to be a one-stop shop for complex and critical mechanical components, at first mostly for the wind sector. It has grown from one machining unit into 14 facilities across 10 locations in and around Pune.

Ultra Corpotech Inc. brings that one-stop solution for specialized mechanical integration to the United States. Whether you need a single machined part or a fully fabricated and finished assembly, we are here to give you that Ultra Edge.

Our Journey

  1. 1991

    Ultra Engineers is founded

    Ashok N. Bhosle starts the company in Pune, making dies and moulds, precision components, jigs and fixtures.

  2. 2002

    CNC machining for energy

    A CNC and VMC facility opens to serve the energy sector.

  3. 2008

    Into oil and gas

    Machined components for the pumping and drilling side of the industry.

  4. 2013

    Coatings and threading

    Molykote, zinc and manganese phosphate coatings, and approval for proprietary threadings.

  5. 2015

    Earth-moving equipment

    A dedicated unit for earth-moving equipment opens in Chakan.

  6. 2019

    Laser cladding and brazing

    Two new special processes join the group’s surface engineering.

  7. 2022

    Galvanizing in-house

    A new hot-dip galvanizing plant, with expanded fabrication and machining.

  8. 2025

    Fabrication up to 50 tons

    A new plant in Nighoje, Chakan, adds robotic welding, heavy fabrication and painting for parts up to 50 tons.

  9. Today

    Ultra Corpotech Inc.

    Our US subsidiary in Conroe, Texas, brings the group to customers across the United States.
