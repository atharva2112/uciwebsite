What We Deliver

All capabilities

  • Fabrication

    Laser, plasma and oxy-fuel cutting, CNC bending, and SMAW, GMAW, GTAW, SAW and robotic welding, from 1 mm sheet to plate over 250 mm thick.

  • Machining

    More than 450 CNC turning, VMC, HMC, VTL, mill-turn and grinding machines, for parts from 50 g to over 30 t.

  • Surface treatment

    Shot blasting, painting, powder coating, hot-dip galvanizing, glass beading, CED coating and zinc plating.

  • Engineering and quality

    In-house fixture design and tooling, first article development, NDT, inspection and full quality documentation.

  • Logistics

    Domestic and export shipping, warehousing and third-party logistics, from the plant to your dock.
