Our Process Chain

Six stages, one group, and no hand-offs between suppliers.

  • Cutting and forming

    Profiles cut, pressed, rolled and formed, ready for welding or machining.

    • Laser cutting
    • Plasma cutting
    • Oxy-fuel cutting
    • CNC bending
    • CNC pressing and rolling
    • Roll forming

  • Welding and fabrication

    Welded fabrications and structures, with heavy fabrication for parts up to 50 t.

    • SMAW, GMAW, GTAW and SAW welding
    • Robotic welding
    • Welders qualified to AWS and EN standards
    • Gantry lines and overhead cranes

  • Machining

    More than 450 CNC machine tools across the group, for parts from 50 g to over 30 t.

    • CNC turning
    • Vertical machining centers (VMC)
    • Horizontal machining centers (HMC)
    • Vertical turret lathes (VTL)
    • WFL mill-turn machines
    • Grinding
    • Approved proprietary threadings

  • Special processes

    Surface preparation, coatings, plating and heat treatment, applied before your parts ship.

    • Shot blasting
    • Glass beading
    • Liquid painting
    • Powder coating
    • CED coating
    • Hot-dip galvanizing
    • Zinc plating
    • Copper plating
    • Electroless nickel plating (ENP)
    • Zinc and manganese phosphating
    • Molykote coating
    • Ceramic coating
    • Xylan coating
    • Baked-on fluoropolymer coating
    • Heat treatment
    • Laser cladding
    • Brazing

  • Engineering and quality

    Preparing your part for production, then proving it.

    • In-house fixture design
    • Tooling development
    • First article development
    • Quality lab and inspection facility
    • Non-destructive testing (NDT)
    • Pressure testing
    • Inspection and quality documentation

  • Warehousing and logistics

    Fully tested sub-assemblies that fit straight onto your line, delivered from the plant to your dock.

    • Tested sub-assemblies
    • Warehouse partners in North America and Europe
    • Vendor-managed inventory
    • Domestic and export shipping
    • Third-party logistics (3PL)

From Drawing to Delivery

01 / 05

  1. 01

    Engineering review

    We check manufacturability and plan fixtures, tooling and material to US or EU grades.

  2. 02

    First article

    We develop and inspect the first article for your approval.

  3. 03

    Production

    Cutting, fabrication and machining across the group’s plants.

  4. 04

    Finishing and testing

    Coatings and heat treatment, then NDT, pressure testing and inspection, with full documentation.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    Parts ship to your dock or to a warehouse partner near you, with vendor-managed inventory if you need it.
