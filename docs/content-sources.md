# Website content sources

The website represents **Ultra Corpotech Inc. (UCI), Conroe, Texas**, the American subsidiary of Ultra Corpotech Pvt Ltd. UCI is the customer's point of contact for projects and deliveries across the United States and Canada. The Pune manufacturing network, group scale and group product experience support that proposition; they are not all located at the Texas site.

The latest supplied source is **UCPL Corporate Presentation -June- 2026.pdf**, a 90-page corporate presentation. Page references below use the PDF's one-based page order. This document records the factual basis for the website update; it does not make the presentation available as a public download.

Earlier material from the December 2025 corporate presentation and [the group website](https://ultracorpotech.com/) remains where it was not replaced or contradicted by the newer source. The original solar tracker, aluminum and hardware examples, much of the facility photography and some process detail come from those earlier sources. Do not attribute every website image or claim to the June 2026 file.

### Image treatment and viewer

For the 22 upgraded or added images, native embedded photographs were extracted from the PDF before background removal and clarity cleanup with image generation. This retains a better source than a screenshot of a complete slide. The original PDF's soft masks often only round the photograph's corners; they do not remove the factory or workshop background.

The resulting transparent images use the site's existing turntable, tilt, sheen and wireframe treatment. An Inspect control opens an enlarged component view and displays available example specifications. For these 22 images, `assets/img/parts/detail/` now contains lossless WebP conversions of the existing full-resolution generated cutouts, selected through `data-full-src`. This restores the available detail from those files without another generation or upscale. The Flowcell (`og-surface-04`) and Y-block (`og-surface-10`) also have larger actual photographs from page 59 (xrefs 779 and 781, respectively) as inspector sources; they show different views of the components and retain their photographic backgrounds. The inspector caps images at their natural resolution. The later quality pass restored all 79 legacy component references to improve clarity and regenerated their matching edge masks. [Product restoration records](product-image-restorations.json) document those 79 sources and accepted outputs. Two members of this restored set—the Flowcell and Y-block—retain the actual page-59 photographs described above as their inspector sources. These restored visuals must remain distinguished from the original photographs: generation can reconstruct detail that was not resolved in a small source. This is a visual presentation of component photography, **not a dimensionally accurate CAD model or a 360-degree reconstruction**. Image cleanup can change fine surface details; use approved engineering drawings for geometry, tolerances, material and acceptance requirements. Source dimensions and weights describe the pictured example, not every product in the family.

[Image-quality source records](image-quality-sources.json) document the earlier inspector files and lossless conversions of available source images. The current Facilities gallery uses **13 AI-restored shop-floor and machine photographs** instead of relying on those conversions for visual improvement. [Facility restoration records](facility-image-restorations.json) identify every original, restored output, dimensions and review notes. Responsive 800-pixel and full-resolution WebP files are accompanied by JPEG fallbacks. The displayed images preserve the full composition without the previous gallery crops. Their enlarged viewer labels them as AI-enhanced and links to the unchanged original JPEG for comparison.

The facility restorations were reviewed against the supplied photographs for the main machine and scene composition. Fine surface features, lettering, tools and other small details may be inferred. The photographs illustrate the documented facilities and equipment; restored detail does not establish a machine specification, inspection result or additional capability. Where greater documentary fidelity is needed, replace the enhancement with an approved full-resolution original. The two aerial photographs are excluded from this restoration treatment.
