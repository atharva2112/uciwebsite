Why Build a Career at Ultra

  • Work on critical parts

    Components for wind turbines, railways, oil and gas, and telecom networks, made in 14 plants with more than 550 CNC machine tools and 30 welding robots.

  • Learning every year

    Continual training for every employee, through UCU, the group’s learning and development program, and a training school for operators and workers.

  • International exposure

    Customers across North and South America, Europe, Australia, the Middle East, Africa and Asia-Pacific, and colleagues in India and the United States.

  • Equal opportunity

    An equal opportunity employer, with a safe, pollution-free workplace and a long-term view of its people.
