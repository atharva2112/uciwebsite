Sector focus

Metro and Railway

The group supplies fully fabricated and machined components to global transportation companies, national railway and metro projects, and global locomotive manufacturers.

Parts range from 0.5 kg to 4,500 kg (about 1.1 lb to 9,900 lb), in carbon and alloy steel to US, EU and other global grades, with fabrication to EN 15085-2 (CL1).

Typical parts

  • Main cabins
  • Auxiliary cabins
  • Front and rear end upper frames
  • Headstocks
  • Bogie frames
  • Consoles
  • Antenna brackets
